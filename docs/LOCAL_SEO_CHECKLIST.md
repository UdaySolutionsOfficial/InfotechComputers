## 4. Authentic Photography & Visual Assets Guide

Google rewards businesses that upload genuine, high-resolution photos taken on-site over generic stock photos.

### Photography Guidelines for Institute Staff:
1. **Exterior & Entrance** (2–3 photos):
   * Clear daylight photograph of the center entrance and official sign board.
   * Street view showing the building so students can find the location easily.
2. **Computer Lab & Desks** (3–5 photos):
   * Clean, organized computer lab setup with monitors, keyboards, and comfortable seating.
   * Wide-angle shot of the classroom during study hours.
3. **Faculty & Counseling Desk** (2 photos):
   * Reception / inquiry desk where students and parents receive guidance.
   * Factual representation without artificial filters.
4. **Avoid**:
   * Stock images from foreign technology websites.
   * Blurry, low-light photos.
   * Watermarked or text-overlaid promotional flyers.
